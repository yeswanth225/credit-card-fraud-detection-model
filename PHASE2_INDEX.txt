CORE MODULES (phase2/quantum/)
================================================================================

config.py (200 lines)
  - QuantumConfig dataclass with all parameters
  - DEFAULT_CONFIG: 4-qubit, 800 train samples
  - CONFIG_8Q: 8-qubit variant with reduced samples

data_preparation.py (320 lines)
  - Load Phase 1 quantum dataset (227k samples, 8 features)
  - Balanced/stratified subsampling
  - Re-scale to [-pi, pi] for quantum encoding

feature_encoding.py (240 lines)
  - Angle encoding (baseline)
  - ZZ feature map (proven for kernels)
  - Pauli feature map (generalization)

quantum_kernel.py (170 lines)
  - FidelityQuantumKernel builder
  - Kernel matrix computation with timing

circuits.py (180 lines)
  - Circuit resource analysis
  - Cost estimation and utilities

evaluation.py (250 lines)
  - Fraud-specific metrics (PR-AUC primary)
  - ROC-AUC, F1, Precision, Recall, FPR

qsvc_model.py (280 lines)
  - Quantum SVC implementation
  - End-to-end pipeline

vqc_model.py (380 lines)
  - Variational Quantum Classifier
  - RealAmplitudes ansatz + training
